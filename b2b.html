FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the content CBSE aligned? ▾
Yes — all content is fully aligned with the CBSE curriculum, including the AI subject (Code 417) for Classes 9 and 10. The Courses for Classes 6–8 follow a CBSE-appropriate difficulty progression.
How long does school onboarding take? ▾
Typically 1–2 weeks from signing the agreement. This includes platform setup, teacher accounts, student onboarding, and an initial teacher training session with Nirav.
Do teachers need AI knowledge to teach this? ▾
No prior AI knowledge is required! Nirav Trivedi personally conducts teacher training sessions as part of our school partnership. Teachers receive detailed lesson plans, answer keys, and ongoing support.
Can we customise the curriculum? ▾
Absolutely. For institutional partnerships, we offer custom curriculum mapping to your existing timetable and learning objectives. Contact us and Nirav will personally review your requirements.
What is the minimum cohort size for institutional pricing? ▾
Institutional pricing starts from 50 students for schools. For colleges, we offer per-student pricing from 30 students. Smaller groups can opt for our direct training programmes.
Is there a trial period for schools? ▾
Yes — we offer a 2-week free pilot for serious institutional enquiries, covering up to 30 students. Reach out via the contact form to arrange a pilot.
