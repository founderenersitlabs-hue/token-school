FAQ

Pricing Questions Answered

Is there really no credit card needed for the free trial? ▾
Correct — you can start your 7-day free trial with just an email address. No payment information is required until you decide to continue after the trial.
Can I switch between plans? ▾
Yes — you can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What does "All 7 Classes" mean in Scholar? ▾
Scholar gives you simultaneous access to all AI courses for Classes 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 12. You can study multiple classes at once — great for students who want to get ahead or parents with multiple children.
Are the live sessions in Pro with Nirav Trivedi himself? ▾
Yes — Pro plan includes 4 monthly live video sessions personally with Nirav Trivedi. These are 1-on-1 or small group sessions of maximum 8 students, focused on your specific learning goals.
Is there a group / family discount? ▾
We offer family discounts for 2+ siblings from the same household. Please contact us via the B2B page for a custom family plan.
Do you offer institutional / school pricing? ▾
Yes — institutional pricing starts at ₹299/student/year for schools with 50+ students. Visit our B2B Solutions page or contact us directly for a custom quote.
