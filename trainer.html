About Nirav

The Educator Behind Token School

A technologist, educator, and AI evangelist dedicated to making AI literacy the norm in every Indian school.

🧠

Teaching Philosophy

Nirav believes AI should feel natural, not intimidating. His teaching style combines real-world industry examples, interactive coding, storytelling, and hands-on experiments — ensuring every student from Class 6 to 12 genuinely enjoys AI. "If a student isn't excited, we haven't done our job."

🏗️

Curriculum Design

Every module in Token School was personally designed by Nirav from the ground up. Drawing from his industry experience and deep understanding of CBSE pedagogy, he ensures the curriculum is rigorous yet age-appropriate, building from simple intuition to advanced implementations across 7 classes.

🏭

Industry Background

Before founding Token School, Nirav worked in AI and technology roles across multiple sectors including ed-tech, finance, and manufacturing. His B.Tech and MBA background gives him the unique ability to explain complex AI concepts in both technical and business terms — invaluable for students and institutions alike.

🌍

Mission & Vision

Nirav's mission is simple: ensure every CBSE student graduates with AI literacy, regardless of their city, school, or background. Token School has already reached 12,000+ students across India, and Nirav continues to personally mentor select students and partner with schools to expand this impact.

Experience

Nirav's Journey

2016–2018

B.Tech + Early AI Research

Completed B.Tech in Computer Science with a focus on Machine Learning and neural networks. Began teaching programming and algorithms at university coding clubs.

2018–2020

MBA + EdTech Leadership

Completed MBA with a specialisation in strategy. Led product teams at an ed-tech startup, developing curriculum for 10,000+ students. Identified the massive gap in school-level AI education in India.

2020–2022

AI Curriculum Consultant

Partnered with 15+ schools across Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Delhi to pilot AI programmes. Trained 200+ teachers. Designed the prototype of what would become Token School's curriculum.

2022–2023

Founded Token School

Launched Token School with a clear mission: deliver India's best AI curriculum for CBSE students. Within the first year, partnered with 30 schools and enrolled 5,000+ individual students.

2024–Present

Scaling AI Education Across India

Token School now serves 12,000+ students across 50+ institutions. Nirav continues to personally teach advanced cohorts, train teachers, and design new modules for emerging AI topics like Generative AI and LLMs.
